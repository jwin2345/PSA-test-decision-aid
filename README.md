# PSA Screening Decision Tool (version 1.1)

A calculator for men aged 65–84 who are already being screened. It estimates life expectancy and the risk of metastatic prostate cancer from TriNetX health-system data and the US life table, and indicates whether stopping PSA screening is reasonable.

**Research use only.** The tool has not been validated in patient-level data and is not a substitute for clinical judgment or shared decision-making.

Joshua Winograd, MD, MS, Department of Urology, University of Rochester Medical Center. The calculator code was written with AI assistance.
